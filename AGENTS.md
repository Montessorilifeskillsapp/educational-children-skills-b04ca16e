# Agents
- InstallBanner uses shared auth and route context and only responds to mobile native install prompts; existing dismissal timestamps are permanent. Why: no repeated install interruptions.
- Family Dashboard lives in `src/components/family/`; old demo dashboard is kept unrouted in `src/components/schools-template/` as the future Schools Portal template. Why: parents need real per-child data; schools reuse the richer layout later.
- Dashboard writes (goals, calendar, prefs, push tokens, reports) go through the `dashboard-data` edge function; tables are read-only to clients via owner RLS. Why: project rule of no direct client writes.
- Notifications run from one hourly `family-notifications` cron job (email via send-transactional-email `family-notice`, push via FCM using FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON). Why: single bounded job, per-timezone hour granularity.
- Child allowance is tier-based and enforced by a child_profiles insert trigger: Free/Premium allow one child and Family allows four; legacy add-ons remain readable only during migration. Why: client inserts profiles directly, so limits must be server-authoritative.
- Materials-list thumbnails resolve from a local exact-name item-photo registry, not from affiliate product pages or activity scenes. Why: purchase listings change and a wrong object photo misleads families.
- Native subscriptions use four stable products (`premium_monthly`, `premium_annual`, `family_monthly`, `family_annual`) and preserve independent valid web/store access. Why: tier identity and allowance must not depend on price matching.
- School Portal lives in `src/components/school/` with lazy `/school` and `/admin/schools` routes; school tables are member-read-only via RLS and all writes go through the `school-portal` edge function (role rules in its `rules.ts`). Why: keeps schools isolated from family data and the rest of the app unchanged.
- School invitations use the runtime-independent invitations helper and may carry same-school classroom_ids that are assigned on acceptance; email queue acceptance is separate from saving a school/invite, and acceptance follows a successful membership write. Why: failed email or membership writes must not masquerade as successful onboarding.
- School staff reach the portal from a global corner School Portal button (AdminAnalyticsLink) that calls the school-portal `me` action on sign-in, focus and reconnection and prewarms portal code/data in memory (`portalCache.ts`); NavigationMenu is unmounted. Why: instant portal opening; matching pending invitations must activate without an email click, with server-owned role assignment.

- School observations use a compact activity browser with one shared in-place explicit-save editor and guarded context switching; stored status identifiers remain stable while labels describe observed work. Why: prevents accidental writes and preserves existing records across presentation changes.
- Student dialogs show saved observations and open the shared Record workspace for changes; the portal does not mount the inactivity overlay. Why: keeps classroom work uninterrupted without duplicate editors or weakening data-access checks.
- School data refreshes keep the current classroom mounted after saves but reset on school changes. Why: routine writes must not interrupt work or carry records across school contexts.
- School parent reports are sent one-tap from the teacher's own school email app (mailto) and recorded via `report_record`; schools must have a required school email at onboarding. Why: reports must come from the school's own email network, which the app cannot impersonate.
- School activity guides use shared exact-activity links, URL-based observation context and a single app-wide portal-return bar; portal departures dispatch a cancelable navigation event for draft guards. Why: staff can browse the app and return to their student/activity without silently discarding work; navigation markers never grant access.
- School staff grade from inside curriculum activity pages via a global `ClassroomDrawer` that renders only for school members with an open `?activity=`; it reuses ProgressChooser and the school-portal edge function. Why: one app for curriculum and classroom recording without changing the public/family experience.
- Transactional email (send-transactional-email) sends directly through the linked Gmail connector gateway, not a DB queue. Why: the queue tables never existed and the Lovable sender domain failed verification.
- School staff capabilities and classroom scope come from `schools.policies` resolved by `resolvePolicies` in school-portal `rules.ts` (shared by client and server); School Admins always hold every capability. Why: each school, not the product, decides how much scope teachers and assistants get.
- Child profile writes are School Admin-only regardless of legacy edit_roster policy values; scoped staff open the same roster form read-only. Why: child and parent contact information must remain visible without granting staff editing access.
- The child form splits names locally and joins them into existing full-name fields on save; legacy names split after their first word without dropping the remaining words. Why: name-field presentation must not change stored identifiers or downstream reports.
- Practical Life activities resolve from the single `src/data/practicalLifeAll.ts` map everywhere. Why: separate merge orders let pages and portal links disagree.
- School activity collections retain every area's registry entries, including shared activity IDs; list keys include area and ID while observation IDs stay unchanged. Why: cross-area deduplication hides activities from Record and lesson planning.
- School parent-report style (heading, notes, sections) is stored per school on the device, not on the server. Why: wording preference only; report records still save through report_record.
- School classroom/student reads enforce assignment scope in private-schema RLS helpers; student erasure is a service-only atomic function and input schemas reject unknown fields. Why: UI filtering cannot protect sensitive records, partial deletion leaves personal data behind, and silent field stripping hides failed saves.
- Parent school-record reads use the school-portal parent_records action, requiring a confirmed matching parent email and audited School Admin approval; changing the roster email resets approval. The Family Dashboard renders all child-specific notes, progress, health notes and reports without report-style filters. Why: parent transparency must not expose records to unverified contacts or other families.
- School change history is a paginated School Admin-only view of the append-only audit log, without copying health notes or report contents into audit entries. Why: oversight must not create a second store of student-sensitive data.
- School curriculum-card statuses come from a per-tab focused-student store (`src/components/school/focusStudent.ts`) set by the portal's student panel and render nothing for families or signed-out visitors. Why: at-a-glance status without changing the public app or adding reads.
- 0–3 (Nido/Infant Community) lives in `src/data/curriculumStages.ts`: ids prefixed `it-`, four areas, empty registry behind `INFANT_TODDLER_ENABLED`; plane resolves from id prefix or classroom age group. Why: future 0–3 content is a data-only addition without touching 3–6.
- School notes and progress saved without a connection queue on the device (`offlineQueue.ts`, scoped to the signed-in user) and replay automatically; portal data refreshes on sync, focus and every minute; in-progress text autosaves per user/child via `drafts.ts`. Why: lost Wi-Fi or navigating away must never lose classroom work.
- School planning uses local date-only helpers for Week/Month views and retains existing plan_save/plan_done/plan_delete calls; controls mirror planning and recording permissions independently. Why: calendar presentation must not change stored records or grant new capabilities.
- Portal `useRecordPrivacy`: Android FLAG_SECURE, copy blocking outside fields, static CSS name/date watermark, device-toggle blur when unfocused; printing allowed. Why: deter and trace capture cheaply.
