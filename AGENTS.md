# Agents
- InstallBanner uses shared auth and route context and only responds to mobile native install prompts; existing dismissal timestamps are permanent. Why: no repeated install interruptions.
- Family Dashboard lives in `src/components/family/`; old demo dashboard is kept unrouted in `src/components/schools-template/` as the future Schools Portal template. Why: parents need real per-child data; schools reuse the richer layout later.
- Dashboard writes (goals, calendar, prefs, push tokens, reports) go through the `dashboard-data` edge function; tables are read-only to clients via owner RLS. Why: project rule of no direct client writes.
- Notifications run from one hourly `family-notifications` cron job (email via send-transactional-email `family-notice`, push via FCM using FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON). Why: one bounded job, per-timezone hours.
- Child allowance is tier-based and enforced by a child_profiles insert trigger: Free/Premium allow one child and Family allows four; legacy add-ons remain readable only during migration. Why: client inserts profiles directly, so limits must be server-authoritative.
- Materials-list thumbnails resolve from a local exact-name item-photo registry, not from affiliate product pages or activity scenes. Why: listings change; wrong photos mislead.
- Native subscriptions use four stable products (`premium_monthly`, `premium_annual`, `family_monthly`, `family_annual`) and preserve independent valid web/store access. Why: tier identity and allowance must not depend on price matching.
- School Portal lives in `src/components/school/` with lazy `/school` and `/admin/schools` routes; school tables are member-read-only via RLS and all writes go through the `school-portal` edge function (role rules in its `rules.ts`). Why: keeps schools isolated from family data and the rest of the app unchanged.
- School invitations use the runtime-independent invitations helper and may carry same-school classroom_ids that are assigned on acceptance; email queue acceptance is separate from saving a school/invite, and acceptance follows a successful membership write. Why: failed email or membership writes must not masquerade as successful onboarding.
- Staff detection reads own school_members rows directly (`me` accepts invites in background); staff land on /school; `portalCache.ts` prewarms. Why: no cold-start waits.

- School observations, student dialogs, data refreshes, parent reports, the global `ClassroomDrawer`, child profile writes and the child form: see `src/components/school/AGENTS.md`.
- School activity guides use shared exact-activity links, URL-based observation context and a single app-wide portal-return bar; portal departures dispatch a cancelable navigation event for draft guards. Why: staff can browse the app and return to their student/activity without silently discarding work; navigation markers never grant access.
- Transactional email (send-transactional-email) sends directly through the linked Gmail connector gateway, not a DB queue. Why: the queue tables never existed and the Lovable sender domain failed verification.
- School staff capabilities and classroom scope come from `schools.policies` resolved by `resolvePolicies` in school-portal `rules.ts` (shared by client and server); School Admins always hold every capability. Why: each school, not the product, decides how much scope teachers and assistants get.
- Practical Life activities resolve from the single `src/data/practicalLifeAll.ts` map everywhere. Why: separate merge orders let pages and portal links disagree.
- School activity collections retain every area's registry entries, including shared activity IDs; list keys include area and ID while observation IDs stay unchanged. Why: cross-area deduplication hides activities from Record and lesson planning.
- School classroom/student reads enforce assignment scope in private-schema RLS helpers; student erasure is a service-only atomic function and input schemas reject unknown fields. Why: UI filtering cannot protect sensitive records, partial deletion leaves personal data behind, and silent field stripping hides failed saves.
- Parent school-record reads use the school-portal parent_records action, requiring a confirmed matching parent email and audited School Admin approval; changing the roster email resets approval. The Family Dashboard renders all child-specific notes, progress, health notes and reports without report-style filters. Why: parent transparency must not expose records to unverified contacts or other families.
- School change history is a paginated School Admin-only view of the append-only audit log, without copying health notes or report contents into audit entries. Why: oversight must not create a second store of student-sensitive data.
- School curriculum-card statuses come from a per-tab focused-student store (`src/components/school/focusStudent.ts`) set by the portal's student panel and render nothing for families or signed-out visitors. Why: at-a-glance status without changing the public app or adding reads.
- 0–3 (Nido/Infant Community) lives in `src/data/curriculumStages.ts`: ids prefixed `it-`, four areas, empty registry behind `INFANT_TODDLER_ENABLED`; plane resolves from id prefix or classroom age group. Why: future 0–3 content is a data-only addition without touching 3–6.

- Subdirectories have their own `AGENTS.md`; read it before changing files there.- School staff without their own family children are sent to the School Portal from Start Free and family/child-setup screens; staff who also have children keep their Family Dashboard. Why: invited staff must not create family child profiles by accident.
- Offline: `vite-plugin-pwa` precaches code/CSS/HTML only, registered only via `src/lib/registerOffline.ts` (never dev/preview/iframes); bundled photos are saved gradually into the worker's runtime cache by `src/lib/offlinePhotos.ts` from the build-generated `offline-photos.json`; videos/songs cached on demand by `src/lib/offlineMedia.ts`; Firebase messaging has its own worker scope. Why: full offline use without a first-visit download burst that stalls the app.
- Vite splits stable vendor chunks; screens beyond the home page (including `AppLayout` views and the classroom drawer) load lazily; every <img> uses decoding="async"; photos never downscaled or recompressed. Why: fast start-up, full image quality.
- Consultation bookings (`book-consultation`): save first, then email the owner. Why: no visitor mail app needed.
- Consent: skill `src/lib/consent.ts` + `index.html` defaults before GA; `CookieConsent` only banner; `analytics.track` awaits `canTrack`. Why: one consent source.
- Stripe annual renewal reminders: `_shared/renewalReminder.ts` in `family-notifications`, claimed in `subscription_renewal_notices` first. Why: one reminder per renewal.
- i18n: `src/i18n/`, unprefixed routes; UI text lives in `src/i18n/locales/<namespace>/<lang>.ts` (English source, other languages typed to its shape), assembled per language in `src/i18n/bundles/`; only English ships in the start-up code and other languages load through the i18next backend in `index.ts`; curriculum data keeps English identifiers. Why: small start-up download, offline, links intact, stored data unchanged.
- Lesson content translations live in `src/i18n/lessons/<lang>.json`, keyed by exact English text and applied only at display time (`lessonText`); material names, category keys and `SkillRef.titleEn` stay English. Why: photos, purchase links, standards and styling look up English identifiers.
- Legal pages (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Songs License) are data: English source in `src/content/<doc>/*.en.ts` with inline markup, per-language JSON mirrors loaded on demand, rendered via `src/components/legal/`; tests enforce identical structure, links, emails and numbers, and translations show the English-applies note. Why: legal text must stay complete and in sync across languages.
- User-facing dates use Intl via `formatDate`/`formatDateRange` in `src/i18n/format.ts`, not date-fns patterns. Why: date-fns lacks Swahili and fixed patterns put day and month in English order.
- Family data export and account erasure go through the `account-data` edge function (family-owned tables only; refuses while subscribed or a School Admin). Why: self-serve data rights without touching school-owned records.
- Child photos switch by language via `src/lib/localizedMedia.ts`: a `<name>-sw|hi|zh.jpg` file next to `<name>.jpg` under src/assets/materials is picked up automatically at image render points (cards, activity page, upgrade dialog); en/es/de keep originals. Why: one rule, adding a variant needs no code.
- Homepage "A clearer starting point" questions open `StartingPointGuide` (one step-through dialog, text in `home.problem.guide`, one onward link per step). Why: first-time parents get the answer in place, with the full page one tap away.
- Every Sign Out button calls `signOutAndReset` in `src/lib/signOut.ts`: device-only sign-out, flushes queued school records first, clears visit markers and reloads home. Why: switching accounts on shared devices must not sign other devices out or leave the previous account's data in memory.
- Text color uses only the `foreground` and `muted-foreground` tokens (faded `muted-foreground/NN` only for small hints; homepage dark footer excepted), enforced by `textShades.test.ts`; long-text `prose` pages read the same tokens from tailwind typography config. Why: uniform, theme-aware text instead of drifting fixed greys.
- Password-reset emails land on `/auth/callback?type=recovery` (phone apps via `?native=1` hand-off to the `com.montessorilifeskills.app://auth-callback` scheme, registered in iOS Info.plist and the Android manifest); `src/lib/passwordRecovery.ts` keeps the pending reset device-wide (localStorage, 1 hour), the callback uses the link's own session even over an existing one, `recovery`-stamped sign-ins are exposed only as `recoveryUser`; saving ends all sessions and signs in fresh; and `PasswordRecoveryRedirect` keeps the person on `/auth?reset=1` until they save a password or sign out. Why: a reset link must never leave someone signed in without choosing a new password.
