# Rebuild the homepage around truth and clarity

## What will change
- Keep the existing colour system, brand, section navigation, curriculum access, founder credentials, pricing access, and authentic activity photographs.
- Replace the empty, washed-out opening with a strong photograph-led introduction that immediately identifies the product as an adult preparation guide.
- Remove the phone demonstration and repetitive sales sections that make the page long, static, and contradictory.
- Reorganize the page into a concise progression: adult purpose, what an activity actually contains, curriculum areas, how adults use it, verified founder background, access options, and factual FAQs.
- Add restrained movement through existing scroll reveals and image transitions, while respecting reduced-motion settings.

## Claims audit
- Remove or correct claims about photo-by-photo guidance, daily prompts, guaranteed outcomes, visual growth reports, personalized recommendations, complete video coverage, monthly new activities, ready-made lesson plans, and any other feature not supported by the current app.
- Describe videos and purchase links as available only where provided.
- Describe activity pages accurately: one exact activity photograph, materials guidance, written presentation steps, teaching notes, and protected video where available.
- Preserve verified AMI training and 40 years of experience.
