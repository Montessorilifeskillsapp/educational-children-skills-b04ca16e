# Restructure the homepage around the real product

## What will change
- Keep the current colours, typography, navigation, imagery style, and overall visual identity.
- Keep the new opening wording and proof strip already previewed.
- Replace the existing repetitive sales sections with the clearer sequence below.

## Homepage sequence
1. **The problem** — “Montessori shouldn't require hours of searching,” followed by the seven practical questions and the transition: “Montessori Life Skills puts those answers in your hand.”
2. **Show the product** — rebuild the existing animated preview into a responsive 30–60 second walkthrough: choose activity, review materials, prepare, present, observe, and see what comes next. Follow with “See it. Present it. Observe. Move forward when the child is ready.” and “Try Your First Activity Free.”
3. **Three-step mechanism** — Choose, Present, Progress, ending with the transformation from uncertainty to knowing what to present today.
4. **Three audiences** — Parents, Teachers, and Assistants as three clear selectable cards with the supplied audience-specific wording. The buttons will use existing app destinations for now; separate advertising landing pages are future work, not part of this change.
5. **A real activity** — show Pouring Water using its actual image and content structure: purpose, materials, preparation, presentation, observation, and next activity. Show enough to understand the product, with deeper progression visibly reserved for full access. CTA: “Get All 100+ Activities.”
6. **The curriculum** — lead with “Stop collecting activities. Follow a sequence.” and show the existing curriculum areas visually. Replace the adversarial Pinterest/Google comparison with a factual contrast: scattered ideas versus an organized sequence.
7. **Authority** — retain Kerry Howard's real photograph, verified AMI training, and 40+ years of experience in a concise section with a link to the full biography.
8. **Proof** — do not display a testimonial, school, review, screenshot, or usage claim until verified evidence and permission exist. The homepage will flow directly from authority to pricing rather than showing an empty placeholder.
9. **Pricing** — make Annual the primary option and Monthly secondary. Verify the displayed figures against the live purchase setup before changing them; do not publish a price the checkout does not charge. Keep only supported inclusions and qualify videos or new content where availability varies.

## Accuracy safeguards
- Keep the page unmistakably adult-facing; the child works off-screen with real materials.
- Use “ages 2–6” as requested in the new positioning, while avoiding claims that every individual activity suits every age in that range.
- Do not claim complete video coverage, guaranteed individual outcomes, personalized recommendations, or continuous new content.
- Use existing exact Montessori photographs only; generate no new imagery.
- Preserve the always-visible curriculum navigation and all existing destinations.

## Verification
- Check every CTA and section link.
- Check the walkthrough timing, manual controls, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for overflow, clipped text, and visible navigation.
- Confirm no fabricated or unsupported claims remain.
- Run the homepage tests and the full automated test suite.
