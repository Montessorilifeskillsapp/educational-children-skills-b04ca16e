- School parent-report style (heading, notes, sections) is stored per school on the device, not on the server. Why: wording preference only; report records still save through report_record.
- Portal reads share in-flight requests within the current account, reject replies after account changes, and reconcile settings through draft-aware saved baselines. Why: avoid duplicate loading and lost edits without weakening server permissions.
- School planning uses local date-only helpers for Week/Month views and retains existing plan_save/plan_done/plan_delete calls; controls mirror planning and recording permissions independently. Why: calendar presentation must not change stored records or grant new capabilities.
- Portal `useRecordPrivacy`: Android FLAG_SECURE, copy blocking outside fields; no inactive-window blur; printing allowed; no watermark. Why: records stay clean.
- The U.S. standards crosswalk (`src/components/school/standardsCrosswalk.ts`) is keyed by exact activity title and prints from the portal Curriculum tab; a test fails if any curriculum activity is unmapped or cites an uncataloged code. Why: new activities must not silently lack an alignment.
- School notes and progress saved without a connection queue on the device (`offlineQueue.ts`, scoped to the signed-in user) and replay automatically; portal data refreshes on sync, focus and every minute; in-progress text autosaves per user/child via `drafts.ts`. Why: lost Wi-Fi or navigating away must never lose classroom work.
- School progress recordings are append-only in `student_progress_entries` (read only through school-portal `progress_history`); `student_progress` holds the latest status by recording time, and offline replays carry `recorded_at`. Why: two teachers grading offline must never overwrite each other; School Admins reconcile.
- School automatic backups: School Admin-only `school_backup`; `AutoBackup` (mounted in the portal for real School Admins) builds the AES-encrypted ZIP on the device in `backup.ts` when the current period (`schools.backup_frequency`: quarterly or semester, via `periodOf`) has no backup and a password is saved in that device's localStorage, then `backup_auto_done` audits it and emails the school's contact_email once per period; `family-notifications` sends only a single missed-period email. No front-page backup banner. Why: schools keep their own copy without a second server-side store or nagging.
