---
name: Approval before changes
description: Never change the app without an explicit user request; always keep the section nav bar visible
type: preference
---
Never make unrequested UI/behavior changes — answer questions first, wait for approval.
The top section navigation bar must always be visible (never collapsed behind a hamburger menu).
**Why:** user was upset by unapproved edits and by the nav disappearing at narrow widths.
**How to apply:** discuss before editing; keep section links rendered at all breakpoints.
